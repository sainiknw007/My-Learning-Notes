1). Create a shell script called /home/bob/calculate-price.sh and it must accept 2 numbers as command line parameters $1 and $2. 
It must print the product (multiplied value) of the numbers as output.
But this time in a new format - The total price for items is 1000 dollars
Ans:
bob@caleston-lp10:~$ cat /home/bob/calculate-price.sh
#!/bin/bash

A=$1
B=$2

echo "The  Total price for itesms is $((A*B)) dollars"
bob@caleston-lp10:~$ ./calculate-price.sh 20 30
-su: ./calculate-price.sh: Permission denied
bob@caleston-lp10:~$ chmod +x /home/bob/calculate-price.sh 
bob@caleston-lp10:~$ ./calculate-price.sh 4 5
The  Total price for itesms is 20 dollars
bob@caleston-lp10:~$ bash /home/bob/calculate-price.sh 10 100
The  Total price for itesms is 1000 dollars
